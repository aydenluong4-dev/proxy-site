Harbor Run

You live in the house on the corner, and your car is parked on the driveway. Courier work in a city that never sleeps: grab packages, beat the clock, and keep it clean near the patrol cars. Or don't.

  • E gets you in and out of the car. First person is in the Menu (Esc). On foot, 1-8 picks a hotbar slot and J, Enter or a click fires. Weapons and cars are sold inside shops: walk to a door (red Armory, blue Dealership on the minimap), press E to go in, and use the counter.
  • Park in a lot (blue P signs) for a bonus, and hide from police there.
  • Follow the beam. Cyan is pickup, orange is drop-off. Traffic lights work: cars stop on red, and so should you. Other jobs pop up too: express and fragile-cargo deliveries, courier routes (green), getaway driving, bank heists (gold), street races (purple), taxi fares and car takedowns (red).
  • Crash into traffic or speed past police and the heat rises.
  • Get boxed in and stopped by police and you are busted.
  • Shift for nitro. Drifting refills it. Jump the orange ramps for airtime cash and grab the gold coins.
  • Crashes dent your car. Walls hurt most, lamps and people least. Repairs are done at the Dealership counter, $2 per 1%.
